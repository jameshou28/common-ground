# v2.0 changes

Trained on the v1 profile (`regions-2.0-v1`, cache r3). The training notebook took 39 min, 20 of them fresh data reads; the diagnostics took 17 min. Results are in `model.ipynb` and `diag.ipynb`.

## What changed

- **Library weighting.** Each Moon/Mars library block gets a "commonness": the share of 200 random Earth blocks that match it as well as a typical match from its own region. Matches against common blocks are discounted by `weight x log(1 - commonness)`. The weight is picked from 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 on held-out land, under the rule that region accuracy can't drop more than 0.02.
- **Spatial features.**
  - Two new per-cell features: height against the surroundings at about 0.5 km and 1.5 km.
  - Six power-spectrum bands: the share of relief at each wavelength.
  - Best bowl and best cone fits, each with its radius.
  - Each feature has to pass a planet check before it's used (alone at most 0.55). None failed at full size; in smoke, the finest spectrum band at 4 km did.
- **Joint loss.** Region and landform triplets are trained together (landform triplets weighted 0.5). It's picked when it's within one standard error of the best variant.
- **Landform scores** for each region/landform pair with at least 50 blocks, with a badge only when the landform is validated.
- **Earth floor** searched over 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4. It picked 0.2.
- **Sites: 104 -> 114.**
  - 10 more Apollo training places from NASA SP-2015-626's field-trip appendix.
  - New `landform_tag` column; `region_tag` is now set only where the site really is that terrain.
  - The volcanic fields lost their maria tag and are now tagged "volcanic feature".
  - New kind "training" for geology-training stops (Grand Canyon, Big Bend and so on), used for display only.
- Carried over from v1.3.x: surface out of 100, overall as a geometric mean, overall percentile, site scores on the region scale, contributions in points.

## What worked

- **Weighting was the big win.**

  | Weight | Region accuracy | Analog similarity rank | Poor places in top 25% |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 0 | 0.454 | 68% | 2 |
  | 0.5 | 0.478 | 66% | 2 |
  | 1 | 0.481 | 60% | 2 |
  | 2 (chosen) | 0.489 | 47% | 1 |

  It improved region accuracy as well. Discounting filler blocks also helps tell the regions apart.
- Floor 0.2 and the weighting together cut the landscape rank from 63% to 43%. Overall now puts 55% of analog sites in Earth's top quarter.
- The spatial features didn't give the planet away: no feature failed its check, and the combined planet check at 32 km stayed at 0.68.

## What didn't work

- **Landform scores are effectively off.** Only 4 region/landform pairs had at least 50 blocks, and each region had at most one. The validation test needs two or more landforms per region to compare, so nothing could be validated. No badge will show, which is the safe failure. Fix: lower the minimum to about 25 blocks, and validate a landform by whether its own blocks beat the region's untagged blocks, instead of beating other landforms. That only reruns the landform cell, no retraining.
- **The 4 km planet check rose slightly** (notebook check 0.65 -> 0.68; balanced 0.633 -> 0.653, inside the +0.02 tolerance).
- **The crater test got a little worse** (0.18 -> 0.14, against a base rate of 0.19). The bowl feature didn't make Earth craters match lunar craters. The volcano-region test improved to about chance (0.23 against 0.25).
- **Mars lowlands and volcanic** analog sites still rank poorly (65-68%).
- **Raising the 4 km share to 40% now costs 0.025** region accuracy (0.489 -> 0.464).

## v2.0.1: feature scores and score breakdowns (no retraining)

`regions-2.0.1-v1`. The training rerun came from cache and every number above is unchanged (weight 2, floor 0.2, planet check 0.653 / 0.678, negative set PASS). Only the landform step and the runtime changed.

### Landform (feature) scores

- Minimum blocks per landform 50 -> 25: 4 -> 34 landforms, and every region has at least one.
- New validation: a landform is validated when it scores its own held-out blocks above the region's untagged blocks (AUC at least 0.65, at least 10 blocks tested). The old test ("is it the region's top landform?") couldn't run with one landform per region, and it's still in the table as `top_acc`.
- 11 of 34 validated:

  | Region | Validated (AUC) |
  | --- | --- |
  | Lunar highlands | wrinkle ridge 0.99, crater floor 0.77, sinuous rille 0.77, graben 0.71 |
  | Lunar basins | crater floor 0.65 |
  | Mars highlands | crater rim 0.81, volcanic edifice 0.75 |
  | Mars lowlands | crater rim 0.88 |
  | Mars volcanic | crater rim 0.84, volcanic edifice 0.68 |
  | Mars outflow | yardang 0.73 |

- Several have a high AUC but fewer than 10 tested blocks, so they stay unvalidated: maria crater chain 0.95 (5 tested), maria wrinkle ridge 0.90 (9), outflow mesa 0.95 (9), volcanic scarp 0.89 (6). More tagged library blocks would settle these.
- At chance or worse: maria sinuous rille 0.50, highlands massif 0.13, the south pole crater rim and small crater (0.43 each), Mars highlands crater floor and mesa. These still get a score, but never a badge.
- Each region now shows its best validated landform, falling back to the best unvalidated one (marked as such). Before this, maria showed "sinuous rille" for Meteor Crater even though that landform tests at chance.
- Earth sites with a landform label: their own landform comes out on top for 3 of 8 (Dry Falls channel, Mauna Loa volcanic edifice, Rio Grande Gorge sinuous rille).

### Score breakdowns

The bars under "why it matches" used to be logit points, or logit points times the local slope, so they didn't add up to anything. Each region, landform and closest place now has a `breakdown` in score points that adds up exactly to the score shown:

    score = base + sum(terms) + common + earth

- `terms`: one per measurement. Each scale's step from the top-k aggregate to the calibrated score is replaced by its average slope from a typical pair to this one, and the same goes for the step from logit to the 0-100 score.
- `common`: the library weighting (matches against terrain that's common on Earth count less).
- `earth`: the Earth factor (score minus similarity).
- `base`: the score of a typical match with every measurement at its typical value (about 17 for regions, 21-26 for landforms).
- Checked on Amazon, Meteor Crater and Holuhraun: the parts add up to within 1e-14.
- Plain scaling (multiplying the bars so they add up to the score) doesn't work because the bars have both signs. When they nearly cancel, the scale factor blows up and can flip every sign.

### Cell points (the numbered dots)

- Old: `cell_cost` = the squared distance between each cell and its paired cell, with every feature weighted the same. The 3 lowest were always shown green and the 3 highest red, even on a terrible match.
- New: `cell_points` (8 x 8) is each cell's share of the closest-place score, in score points. A cell feature's input is the mean squared mismatch over the 64 pairs, so its contribution is split evenly over the cells, then shifted by the cell's own mismatch times the local slope of that feature's curve. The cells add up exactly to the 4 km cell features' terms.
- The site shows the 3 highest and 3 lowest. Green means positive, red negative, and grey means the cell is among the lowest but still positive. On good matches most cells are positive (Holuhraun: 63 of 64).

### Speed

Clicks now take about 0.75 s to score (was 0.43 s). That's the 34 landforms, not the breakdowns. Cutting `LF_SHORT` from 30 to 15 would get most of it back if it matters.

### Lined-up match view

- `analog_readers.library_block_dem(..., pad_px=)` can keep up to 2 km (64 px) of margin around a library block. The API's `/library/{index}?pad=true` serves it (branch `library-pad`), and the site lines the block up with the clicked one: the 8 turns and flips, every shift within 2 km, and the best detrended correlation. Display only; scores don't change.
- Meteor Crater against its closest maria block: correlation 0.01 as cut, 0.90 lined up (mirrored, moved 1.9 km). With 1 km of margin it only reached 0.06, since the Moon crater sat about 2 km off-centre.
