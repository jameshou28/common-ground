# v2.0 changes

Trained on the v1 profile (`regions-2.0-v1`, cache r3). The training notebook took 39 min, 20 of them fresh data reads; the diagnostics took 17 min. Results are in `model.ipynb` and `diag.ipynb`.

## What changed

- **Library weighting.** Each Moon/Mars library block gets a "commonness": the share of 200 random Earth blocks that match it as well as a typical match from its own region. Matches against common blocks are discounted by `weight x log(1 - commonness)`. The weight is picked from 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 on held-out land, under the rule that region accuracy can't drop more than 0.02.
- **Spatial features.**
  - Two new per-cell features: height against the surroundings at about 0.5 km and 1.5 km.
  - Six power-spectrum bands: the share of relief at each wavelength.
  - Best bowl and best cone fits, each with its radius.
  - Each feature has to pass a planet check before it's used (alone at most 0.55). None failed at full size; in smoke, the finest spectrum band at 4 km did.
- **Joint loss.** Region and landform triplets are trained together (landform triplets weighted 0.5). It's picked when it's within one standard error of the best variant.
- **Landform scores** for each region/landform pair with at least 50 blocks, with a badge only when the landform is validated.
- **Earth floor** searched over 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4. It picked 0.2.
- **Sites: 104 -> 114.**
  - 10 more Apollo training places from NASA SP-2015-626's field-trip appendix.
  - New `landform_tag` column; `region_tag` is now set only where the site really is that terrain.
  - The volcanic fields lost their maria tag and are now tagged "volcanic feature".
  - New kind "training" for geology-training stops (Grand Canyon, Big Bend and so on), used for display only.
- Carried over from v1.3.x: surface out of 100, overall as a geometric mean, overall percentile, site scores on the region scale, contributions in points.

## What worked

- **Weighting was the big win.**

  | Weight | Region accuracy | Analog similarity rank | Poor places in top 25% |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 0 | 0.454 | 68% | 2 |
  | 0.5 | 0.478 | 66% | 2 |
  | 1 | 0.481 | 60% | 2 |
  | 2 (chosen) | 0.489 | 47% | 1 |

  It improved region accuracy as well. Discounting filler blocks also helps tell the regions apart.
- Floor 0.2 and the weighting together cut the landscape rank from 63% to 43%. Overall now puts 55% of analog sites in Earth's top quarter.
- The spatial features didn't give the planet away: no feature failed its check, and the combined planet check at 32 km stayed at 0.68.

## What didn't work

- **Landform scores are effectively off.** Only 4 region/landform pairs had at least 50 blocks, and each region had at most one. The validation test needs two or more landforms per region to compare, so nothing could be validated. No badge will show, which is the safe failure. Fix: lower the minimum to about 25 blocks, and validate a landform by whether its own blocks beat the region's untagged blocks, instead of beating other landforms. That only reruns the landform cell, no retraining.
- **The 4 km planet check rose slightly** (notebook check 0.65 -> 0.68; balanced 0.633 -> 0.653, inside the +0.02 tolerance).
- **The crater test got a little worse** (0.18 -> 0.14, against a base rate of 0.19). The bowl feature didn't make Earth craters match lunar craters. The volcano-region test improved to about chance (0.23 against 0.25).
- **Mars lowlands and volcanic** analog sites still rank poorly (65-68%).
- **Raising the 4 km share to 40% now costs 0.025** region accuracy (0.489 -> 0.464).
