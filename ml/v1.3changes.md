# v1.3 changes

What changed after v1.2 and how it did. Trained on the v1 profile (`regions-1.3-v1`); results are in `model.ipynb` and `diag.ipynb`.

## Landscape score: similarity x Earth factor

v1.2's landscape score only measured how similar a place is to a Moon or Mars region, so flat farmland scored high on the flat regions (maria, lowlands, outflow plains). Now each region's score is:

    landscape = similarity x (floor + (1 - floor) x Earth percentile)

- Earth percentile = how much more similar this place is to that region than random Earth land (2,500 blocks, stored per region in `model.npz`).
- The factor can only lower a score, never raise a weak match, so the number is still mostly "how much it looks like the Moon/Mars".
- The floor is picked on held-out sites from 0.4-1.0, with the Earth part kept under half of the score. It picked 0.4; the Earth part is 36% of the score.
- Each region's shown score is the one the regions are sorted by, so the UI order and the numbers always agree.
- The response gets `similarity`, `earth_percentile` and `earth_factor` per region, next to `score`.

## Conditions

- New input "sparse vegetation" = WorldCover shrub + grass + moss (the reader used to drop these). Both signs were tested: as a penalty it ranks held-out sites at 27.9%, as a bonus at 33.0%. Kept as a penalty.
- Fixed building penalty: -3 logit points at 5% built-up land or more. The data couldn't learn it, because analog sites have no buildings.
- Trained on terrain and habitat sites only (not rock-sample sites).
- Old cached Earth blocks got their WorldCover shares re-read for the new input (42 s, land cover only).

## Site list

`analog_sites.csv` has three new columns:

- `kind`: terrain (77), habitat (3: MDRS, FMARS, HI-SEAS), sample (24: PTAL rock sites, salt flats, hot springs, lakes).
- `coord_quality`: exact (12, from the ESA catalogue), site (70, a named feature), area (22, a town or region centre).
- `region_tag`: which of the 8 regions the site is an analog for. This is our reading of each source's description, marked in `tag_basis`.

How they're used: conditions trains on terrain + habitat; the landscape test uses terrain sites; everything stays on the website.

## New tests

- Region tags: each terrain site's rank in its own tagged region.
- Standing negative set: 16 places that should never look like good analogs (cities, farmland, rainforest, deltas). Pass = none in the top 25% of random land.
- Area sampling: area-quality sites are also read at 8 points 4 km out, and the evaluation uses the median of the 9 blocks. Evaluation only; live clicks are still one block.

## Results (v1.2 -> v1.3)

Held-out terrain analog sites, median rank among random land (0% = top):

| Score | v1.2 | v1.3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Similarity only | 72% | 70% |
| Landscape | (= similarity) | 63% |
| Conditions | 27% | 28% |
| Overall | 30% | 28%, 43% of sites in the top 25% |

- Negative set: PASS, 0 of 16 in the top 25%. All 16 sit in the bottom 25% of random land on overall.
- Featured-site order (Spearman): conditions 0.48 -> 0.60, Moon 0.56 -> 0.65, Mars 0.50 -> 0.51.
- Region tags: median own-region rank 37%. Moon highlands sites rank at 1% in their region, Mars highlands 24%, volcanic 36%, lowlands 41%, maria 45%. The site's own region is its best region 13% of the time (chance about 1/8).
- Unchanged, as expected (the terrain model wasn't retrained): region accuracy 0.38 / 0.48 / 0.47, planet check 0.65 / 0.69, cross-world tests at chance.
- `model.npz` 11.3 MB, `score_click` 362 ms.

## What's still weak

- The Earth factor helped less than the earlier test suggested (70% -> 63%, not 70% -> 34%). That test used the Earth percentile alone; option C keeps similarity as the main part on purpose. On landscape alone, flat farmland still scores high (Iowa 82, Punjab 77); it's the conditions score that pushes it to the bottom. The floor sat at the edge of the grid (0.4), so 0.2-0.3 is worth trying while keeping the Earth part under half.
- Maria and lowlands sites rank mid-table in their own region. The maria-tagged ones are volcanic fields and cinder cones, which are rougher than real mare plains, so the tag itself may be off.
- Only one site is tagged Mars outflow (Dry Falls) and one Moon basins (Sudbury), too few to say anything.
- `diag.ipynb` section D8 still ranks raw similarity, not the new score. The numbers above come from `model.ipynb`.
- Sites are still 104 (69 used). More sourced sites is the next data job.
