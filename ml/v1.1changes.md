# v1.1 changes

What changed between the first smoke model (regions-1.0) and v1.1, and why. Diagnostics behind each change are in `diag.ipynb`.

## Terrain features (13 -> 7)

The planet check was high (you could guess the planet from a block's features), and the diagnostics showed most of that came from two features that mostly measure DEM noise (fine roughness, curvature). Most of the rest were near-duplicates of slope (46 pairs correlated above 0.9). So:

- Dropped: rough_fine, curv, slope_p90, cell_relief, svf, geo_flat, and the hypsometry block feature.
- Kept: slope_mean, rough_coarse, geo_convex, geo_concave, shadow_low, shadow_mid, norm_height, plus relief, hurst, linearity, depression area and depth.
- On the old data this cut the planet check by about 0.07 without losing region info.

Tried and rejected: FABDEM (bare Earth ground already had the lowest noise, so trees and buildings weren't the problem), blurring (it removes real slope, especially on Mars), and per-world normalisation (made the planet check worse).

## Overall score

The learned blend set the landscape weight to 0, so "overall" was really a desert detector and the same for every region. Now:

    overall = landscape score x (0.4 + 0.6 x conditions / 100)

Landscape always counts; bad site conditions can only pull it down, to as low as 40%. The response has a new `conditions.discount` field.

## Moon south pole

Was 1 block, because Kaguya is striped near the pole and the global LOLA map can't be windowed there. Now south of 80°S reads the polar LOLA DEMs (20 m and 80 m, polar stereographic) from NASA PGDA, sampled up to 89°S, with no stripe check (stripes are a Kaguya thing). Smoke: 60 blocks.

## Mars sampling

Candidates were tested against CTX bounding boxes, but the DTMs are thin slanted strips, so about 75% of reads hit no-data. Now we use the real footprint outlines, require the point to be 4.5 km inside, and allow 5% no-data. On a 40-point test the read success went from 25% to 100%. Smoke: 60 blocks per Mars region (was 16-33).

## Analog sites

- Only our own hand list (61 sites) is used. ShadowAstra's lists were tried in smoke and taken out: they're another team's picks, not a verified source.
- Sites within 10 km of each other are merged into one (Krafla/Námafjall, Cinder Lake/Sunset Crater).
- Sites whose 4 km block is mostly trees, crops, buildings or water get nudged up to 6 km to a cleaner block, or dropped if nothing nearby is clean.
- The hand list's coordinates are still from memory. They need checking against published sources (mission reports, papers) before any conditions number is quoted, and that's the next data job.

## Earth craters

Craters under 1 km are dropped, since they're tiny next to the 1-3 km lunar "small crater" tag. Crater blocks with under 30 m of relief are dropped as buried or eroded.

## Plumbing

- `download_datasets.ipynb` gets the polar LOLA files and the CTX footprints. Its downloader now resumes and checks file sizes, after the first polar download silently stopped at 412 MB of 2.7 GB.
- `PROFILE` defaults to v1 (set `ANALOG_PROFILE=smoke` for a quick check). The cache tag is r2, so old caches aren't reused. The model version is `regions-1.1-<profile>`.
- 24 read threads instead of 12.

## Smoke results (before v1)

| Check | 1.0 smoke | 1.1 smoke |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Region accuracy, site / regional (chance 0.25) | 0.43 / 0.45 | 0.37 / 0.47 |
| Planet check, site / regional (notebook version) | 0.80 / 0.77 | 0.65 / 0.68 |
| Analog sites, overall median rank among random land | 14% (conditions only) | 21% (landscape x discount; smoke still had ShadowAstra sites) |
| Featured-site order, Spearman | 0.46-0.48 | Moon 0.63, Mars 0.36 |
| Earth craters -> crater-tagged Moon matches (base 0.21) | 0.13 | 0.19 |
| Earth volcanoes -> Mars volcanic region (chance 0.25) | 0.13 | 0.13 |

Still open: the volcano transfer, and landscape alone still ranks analog sites below random land (66%). Flat random land matches the flat regions (maria, lowlands, volcanic plains) well. That's now handled by the conditions discount, not by the landscape score itself.
