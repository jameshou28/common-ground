# v1.2 changes

What changed after the v1.1 run, and why. Everything here is in `model.ipynb` but not trained yet. The checks below were run on the v1.1 model (`regions-1.1-v1`); the v1.1 run itself is kept in `model_v1.1_run.ipynb`.

## Analog sites: 56 -> 104, all sourced

`analog_sites.csv` replaces the hand list. A site is in only if:

- a document names it: ESA's Catalogue of Planetary Analogues (CAFE TN2), NASA's Apollo training history (SP-2015-626), the PTAL papers, ESA PANGAEA or Europlanet's field-site pages, or
- at least 3 papers in Europe PMC name it together with "Mars analog", "lunar analog" or similar.

Coordinates come from the ESA catalogue where it gives them, otherwise from Wikipedia's coordinates for that place. None are from memory. Each row lists its documents, paper count, an example paper and where the coordinates came from.

- 192 candidates checked, 104 kept: 89 named in a document, 15 on papers alone.
- 32 Moon-type, 72 Mars-type.
- Well-known sites that didn't make it because nothing I could fetch names them: Wadi Rum, Kelso and Dumont Dunes, Ramon crater, Arkaroola, Tabernas, Salar de Uyuni, SP Crater, Amboy, most Iceland sub-sites. They need a citation before going in. The paper index is thin on geology, so more source documents is the way to grow this.
- Some coordinates are a town or a region centre (Hanksville, Ouarzazate, Dhofar, Qaidam), so those blocks are "in the area", not on a specific outcrop.
- The notebook still merges sites within 10 km and nudges or drops blocks that are mostly trees, crops, buildings or water, so the working set will be smaller than 104.

## Craters: sourced list

`earth_craters.csv`: 38 craters between 1 and 60 km, locations from the USGS impact-site list, diameters from Wikidata. Replaces the 28 typed from memory. Buried ones still get dropped by the relief filter.

## Conditions score

- Snow and ice aren't penalised any more. The model sees "open" = bare ground + snow and ice.
- WorldCover has no tiles for Antarctica, so the Dry Valleys were scored as "unknown land" (conditions 28). South of 60°S with no land cover now counts as open ground.

## Overall score

Now `landscape × conditions / 100`. The 0.4 floor let flat farmland and cities through. On the unseen-site test:

| Formula | New analog sites, median rank | New sites in top 25% | Poor places in top 25% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| floor 0.4 (v1.1) | 24% | 52% | 3 of 16 (Iowa, Tokyo, Punjab) |
| floor 0 (v1.2) | 19% | 67% | 0 of 16 |
| conditions only | 20% | 68% | 0 of 16 |

## 4 km scale

- Coarse roughness is dropped at 4 km. It was the biggest "which planet is this" giveaway and the pair model wasn't using it. Region accuracy didn't change (0.375 vs 0.373). It's done with a mask, so the cached Moon and Mars libraries are still valid.
- The 4 km scale now gets at least 40% of the landscape score. With the learned weights it had 20%, which worked out to 13% of the score's variation, so clicks a few km apart scored almost the same. At 40% it's 31%. Cost: region accuracy 0.51 -> 0.47 on 480 held-out blocks. `site_share_min` in the config sets this; 0.3 costs about 1 point.
- Best matches are re-ranked with both scales over the top 30 candidates (was top 10).

## Cross-world training off

`cross_weight = 0`. In v1.1 the Earth-to-planet triplets made both transfer tests worse. With the weight at 0, every Earth crater and volcano is a test block. The volcano test now also checks how many of the 10 best Mars matches are tagged volcano or lava flow, and both tests only use fairly bare blocks.

## Speed

- Earth blocks are scored in parallel and saved to `data/cache/earth_scores_*.npz`: 127 s for 2,855 blocks, was 781 s. `diag.ipynb` reads that file instead of scoring them again (17 min -> 2 min for that step).
- The Earth cache is per block now. Changing a site list only reads the new blocks.
- Next run should only need the new Earth blocks plus training, roughly 15-20 minutes.

## Checks on the v1.1 model

South pole: 13 blocks read from both Kaguya and polar LOLA agree on slope, shadows, relief and landform shares (correlation 0.92-1.0). Only coarse roughness disagrees. So the south polar region looks like real terrain, not an instrument effect. Small sample.

Unseen sites: 75 of the new sourced sites that v1.1 never trained on, ranked against 2,500 random land blocks (0% = top):

| Score | Median rank | In top 25% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landscape | 67% | 16% |
| Conditions | 20% | 68% |
| Overall (v1.1 formula) | 24% | 52% |

- Best: Qaidam, Dhofar, White Sands, Holuhraun, Murchison Fjord, Dalhousie Springs, Askja, Simpson Desert.
- Worst: vegetated rock-sample sites (Lofoten, Bletterbach, Vargeão, Gardnos, Rum, Ries), which are analogs for their rocks, not their surface, and the Dry Valleys (the Antarctica gap above).
- Poor places: median rank 60%. Rainforest, taiga and deltas rank at the bottom. Flat farmland and cities get landscape scores of 70-85 on "Mars outflow channels" and were only held back by conditions.

## Still wrong

- The landscape score doesn't pick out analog sites on Earth. It ranks them below typical land (67%), mostly because flat land matches the flat Moon and Mars regions. Right now the overall score works because of conditions. This is the main thing left to fix.
- Conditions barely penalises buildings (Tokyo scores 31, Manhattan 21) and is mostly bare / tree / crop. More positives should help after retraining.
- Region accuracy is still modest: 0.39 at 4 km, 0.46 at 32 km (chance 0.25).
- 104 sites is short of the 120-150 target.
